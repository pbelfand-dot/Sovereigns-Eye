# CC0 medieval ambience

These location beds are meant to make settlements feel occupied between major
events. They are public high-quality Freesound preview encodes converted to Ogg
Vorbis. The source pages designate every recording CC0.

| File | Scene | Creator and source |
|---|---|---|
| `market_medieval_crowd.ogg` | Short medieval market crowd accent | [bolkmar — Medieval Market](https://freesound.org/people/bolkmar/sounds/424790/) |
| `market_busy_crowd.ogg` | Long market chatter and selling | [ilmari_freesound — London market ambience](https://freesound.org/people/ilmari_freesound/sounds/659634/) |
| `tavern_chatter.ogg` | Indistinct indoor voices | [dazzamoo — Tavern ambience](https://freesound.org/people/dazzamoo/sounds/651364/) |
| `village_morning.ogg` | Birds, rooster, wind and distant life | [The_Sound_Side — Village morning](https://freesound.org/people/The_Sound_Side/sounds/331442/) |
| `blacksmith_work.ogg` | Forge and anvil work | [LarsErikErtzgaardRingen — Blacksmith](https://freesound.org/people/LarsErikErtzgaardRingen/sounds/609757/) |
| `fireplace_loop.ogg` | Seamless hearth fire | [myLoop — Fireplace seamless loop](https://freesound.org/people/myLoop/sounds/852107/) |
