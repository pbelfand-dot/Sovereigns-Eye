Thatch & Repair (Sovereign's Eye)

The models in models/, the textures in textures/ (T_Thatch_BaseColor.png,
T_ThatchWorn_BaseColor.png, T_Thatch_Normal.png and T_ThatchKit_BaseColor.png,
painted by source/paint_thatch.py) and the build scripts and Blender source
in source/ were made for Sovereign's Eye and are released under CC0 1.0
Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The models are made to sit on the walls of the Quaternius Medieval Village
MegaKit (Standard), CC0 1.0, kept in
3D/environments/quaternius-medieval-village-standard/ with its own
LICENSE.txt. They use none of its textures.
