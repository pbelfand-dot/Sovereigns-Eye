# Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit — Standard

This is the free Standard edition of the Medieval Village MegaKit, selected as
the primary settlement architecture for Sovereign's Eye. Its textured,
illustrated fantasy style is from the same artist as the selected characters and
animation libraries.

## Included

- 176 modular architecture and prop models
- Balconies, exterior and interior corners
- Flat and rounded doors and frames
- Brick and timber floors
- Plaster and uneven-brick overhangs
- Modular round-tile and wooden roofs
- Exterior and interior stair systems
- Plaster and stone walls with door/window variants
- Windows, shutters, chimneys, fences, vines, wagon, crate, and detail pieces
- 22 shared texture maps

The models remain in the source pack's JSON glTF + BIN + shared-PNG layout. This
avoids embedding the same high-resolution brick, plaster, timber, and roof maps
inside 176 separate GLBs. Keep the complete `models/` folder together when
importing so every model can resolve its paired BIN and shared textures.

## What it supports

This pack is enough to construct varied cottages, workshops, taverns, multi-story
townhouses, streets, courtyards, and dense town blocks. Because it is modular and
grid-based, the same parts can build a tiny starting settlement and later expand
into a large village or urban district without changing art direction.

It is not a complete kingdom kit by itself. A capital still needs monumental
castle/palace pieces, defensive walls and gates, civic landmarks, docks, harbor
props, and multiple ship classes.
