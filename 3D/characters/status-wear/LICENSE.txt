Status Wear (Sovereign's Eye)

The models in models/, the textures in textures/ (T_Wear_*_BaseColor.png,
painted by source/paint_wear.py) and the build scripts in source/ were made
for Sovereign's Eye and are released under CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain
dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The pieces are skinned to the rig of the Quaternius Modular Character
Outfits - Fantasy (CC0 1.0, 3D/characters/quaternius-modular-outfits-fantasy-standard/,
with its own LICENSE.txt); the build reads that rig, and the Quaternius
Universal Base Characters' heads and hair (CC0 1.0), to fit them. No
Quaternius geometry is copied into these models.
