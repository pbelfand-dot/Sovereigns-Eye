# Architecture shortlist for the sleek fantasy direction

The goal is a textured, illustrated fantasy world that matches the cleaner
Quaternius character proportions without drifting into voxel, Minecraft, or
toy-block aesthetics.

## 1. Medieval Village MegaKit — recommended foundation

- Official page: https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalvillagemegakit.html
- 304 textured models
- Modular walls, floors, stairs, roofs, doors, windows, vines, and other pieces
- Grid-based construction with both exterior and interior wall surfaces
- glTF, FBX, OBJ, and Blend formats
- CC0; the free Standard download contains roughly 60–70% of the pack

**Why it fits:** It was made by the same artist as the selected character test,
uses the same modern textured-stylized language, and has enough architectural
variation to build a town rather than repeat a few complete houses.

## 2. Ultimate Modular Ruins — secondary biome

- Official page: https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularruins.html
- 90 textured models
- Modular ruins, dungeon pieces, and props
- FBX, OBJ, and Blend formats
- CC0

**Best use:** abandoned districts, crypts, broken keeps, and exploration spaces.
It is older than the village MegaKit, so it should support the main architecture
rather than define the entire visual style.

## 3. Stylized Nature MegaKit — landscape companion

- Official page: https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html
- 116 textured nature models
- 40 trees, 35 plants and flowers, 27 rocks, grass, and bushes
- Seven swappable leaf varieties
- glTF, FBX, OBJ, and Blend formats
- CC0; the free Standard download contains roughly 60–70% of the pack

**Best use:** forests, village edges, roadsides, gardens, and landmark silhouettes.

## 4. Fantasy Props MegaKit — interiors and lived-in detail

- Official page: https://quaternius.com/packs/fantasypropsmegakit.html
- 211 textured, low-poly props using four shared texture sets
- Furniture, tools, weapons, books, potions, market pieces, and breakables
- glTF, FBX, OBJ, and Blend formats
- CC0; the free Standard download contains roughly 60–70% of the pack

**Best use:** filling homes, workshops, taverns, markets, and quest locations so
the modular town does not feel empty or repetitive.

## Recommended stack

Use **Medieval Village MegaKit** as the architecture base, **Stylized Nature
MegaKit** for terrain dressing, **Fantasy Props MegaKit** for density and story,
and **Ultimate Modular Ruins** only for damaged or ancient areas. This keeps one
coherent art family while still giving the world distinct zones.
