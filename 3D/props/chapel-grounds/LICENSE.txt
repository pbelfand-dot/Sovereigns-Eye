Chapel & Churchyard (Sovereign's Eye)

The models in models/, the texture in textures/ (T_Chapel_BaseColor.png,
painted by source/paint_chapel.py) and the build scripts and Blender source
in source/ were made for Sovereign's Eye and are released under CC0 1.0
Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The models also use textures they do not contain: the wood, iron, stone
and roof-tile sheets of the Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit (Standard),
CC0 1.0, kept in 3D/environments/quaternius-medieval-village-standard/ with
its own LICENSE.txt. They are referenced by relative path, not copied.

The churchyards' wattle hurdles, paled fences and their gates are the Farm
& Field family's own models (3D/props/farm-field), CC0 1.0, placed by the
game; they are not copied here.
