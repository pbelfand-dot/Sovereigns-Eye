Market Square (Sovereign's Eye)

The models in models/ and the build scripts and Blender source in source/
were made for Sovereign's Eye and are released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The models also use textures they do not contain: the stone, wood and iron
trim sheets of the Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit (Standard), CC0 1.0,
kept in 3D/environments/quaternius-medieval-village-standard/ with its own
LICENSE.txt, and the Village Commons banner atlas (for the Town cross's
gilding), CC0 1.0, in 3D/props/village-commons/textures/. They are
referenced by relative path, not copied.
